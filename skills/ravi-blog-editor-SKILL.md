# Ravi's Blog Content Editor

A skill for polishing rough blog content and expanding it into fully-formed posts that maintain Ravi's distinctive punchy, humorous voice.

## What This Skill Does

When you hand over draft blog content—whether it's a scattered paragraph, a skeleton outline, or a rough narrative—this skill will:

1. **Fix typos, grammar, and clarity issues** without changing your voice
2. **Improve structure and formatting** for better readability
3. **Expand thin sections** with witty elaboration, examples, or storytelling
4. **Suggest enhancements** that maintain your comedic timing and intellectual depth
5. **Preserve your unique tone**—self-deprecating, observational, reference-heavy, and delightfully tangential

## Understanding Your Voice

Your writing style is built on:

- **Punchy, conversational syntax**: Short sharp sentences mixed with flowing narratives ("Amusing. For the mongrel." followed by detailed explanation)
- **Self-aware humor**: Casual admission of flaws, lazy tendencies, and slow thinking
- **Parenthetical asides**: Extra context, jokes, and clarifications tucked into explanatory parentheses
- **Pop culture and gaming references**: Weaving Half-Life, Ghostbusters, and other cultural touchstones into ordinary moments
- **Whimsical storytelling**: Turning mundane events into elaborate narratives (a park visit becomes a supernatural investigation)
- **Intellectual playfulness**: References feel natural, not forced—they're how you think
- **Observational wit**: Noticing the absurd or funny in everyday moments and highlighting it
- **Scene-setting and timestamps**: Creating narrative structure and immersion ("25th August 2013. Joggers park. 8 am.")

## How to Use This Skill

**Provide your draft content** (rough, polished, anywhere in between).

**Specify what you want help with** (optional, but helpful):
- "Just fix typos and tighten it"
- "Expand this with more detail"
- "Make it punchier"
- "Add more humor where it naturally fits"
- "Fix the structure and suggest improvements"

**What you'll get back:**
- A corrected, expanded, polished version ready for your blog
- Inline suggestions for improvements (which sections could use more wit, where pacing drags, etc.)
- A short notes section highlighting key changes and why they work for your voice

## Example Transformation

**Your draft:**
> I went to the park and saw a dog. It was funny. Then I took a picture and thought it looked like a Ghostbuster scene.

**What the skill does:**
> Afternoon at Jogger's Park. I'd wandered over on a whim, camera in hand, when this scrappy golden retriever barreled toward his owner—who was attempting, with limited success, to teach the poor thing how to sit. Amusing. For the dog, at least. The whole scene had this chaotic energy that made me think: *what if I framed this differently?* I snapped a few shots, and when I opened them in editing, one jumped out—the light, the motion, the absurdity of it all. It looked exactly like something the Ghostbusters would flag.

## What This Skill Won't Do

- Change your core ideas or narrative arc
- Force a different tone (no making it overly formal or stilted)
- Add content that doesn't fit your voice
- Rewrite sections wholesale without explaining why
- Ignore what you're trying to say in favor of what sounds "better"

## When This Skill Triggers

This skill should activate whenever you:
- Share rough blog content for polishing
- Ask to expand a draft into a full post
- Request typo/grammar fixes with style improvements
- Want formatting suggestions for a piece
- Need help tightening or expanding specific sections
- Ask for feedback on structure or pacing
- Request enhancement suggestions while keeping your voice intact

## The Process

1. **Read through the draft** and understand the core idea, story, or point you're making
2. **Identify typos, grammar issues, and clarity problems** and fix them
3. **Assess structure**: Does it flow? Do sections drag? Is the pacing right?
4. **Expand thin areas** by:
   - Adding sensory details and scene-setting
   - Elaborating on observations with wit
   - Weaving in relevant references where natural
   - Using parenthetical asides for humor and context
   - Building out underdeveloped narratives
5. **Enhance with style**:
   - Sharpen sentence structure (mix punchy lines with flowing narrative)
   - Add observational humor where it fits naturally
   - Strengthen self-aware commentary
   - Integrate pop culture references if they enhance meaning (not for the sake of it)
6. **Suggest improvements** for sections that could punch harder or flow better
7. **Preserve your voice throughout** — this is *you*, just sharper

## Key Style Principles to Keep

- Use short, sharp sentences to create emphasis and comedic timing
- Mix in longer, flowing sentences for narrative and explanation
- Parentheses are your friend — use them for asides, humor, and extra context
- References should feel organic to how you think, not shoehorned in
- Self-deprecating humor works because you genuinely believe it
- Timestamps and scene-setting create immersion (use liberally)
- Whimsy is good — unexpected narrative turns that feel natural are great
- Intellectual depth should hide beneath the humor, not show off above it
