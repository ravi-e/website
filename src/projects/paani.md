**Paani** (the Hindi word for water) is a mobile application built specifically with accessibility and senior usability in mind. Its primary goal is to prevent dehydration in senior citizens by simplifying water intake tracking.

## Core Features

* **High Contrast UI:** Complies with WCAG AA accessibility standards to ensure text and buttons are highly visible to users with low vision.
* **Large Touch Targets:** Oversized interactive components reduce accidental misclicks and make tracking comfortable for seniors with mild motor tremors.
* **Audio Feedback:** Optional auditory confirmations for successful tracking actions.

## User-Centered Design Process

Senior interfaces require a rejection of standard "bubbly, complex dashboard" structures in favor of extreme clarity:

1. **One-Action Logging:** Logging water intake takes a single tap from the home screen—no navigation menus or input fields.
2. **Context-Aware Reminders:** Notifications are timed around senior daily schedules (morning, mid-day, evening) rather than generic hourly intervals.
3. **No Login Walls:** The app runs entirely offline, keeping user data private and removing the friction of remembering usernames or passwords.
