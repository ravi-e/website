**EndoQuiz** is a Progressive Web Application (PWA) designed to aid dental students in their licensing exam preparation, specifically focusing on endodontic questions and board preparation.

## Features

- **Offline Support:** Full service worker integration enables students to take practice exams in locations with poor or no internet coverage (e.g. transit or clinics).
- **Spaced Repetition:** Implementation of a lightweight client-side algorithm to track weak topic areas and surface relevant questions more frequently.
- **Responsive Layout:** Adaptive styling optimized for single-handed use on mobile viewports.

## Process & Challenges

Creating EndoQuiz required translating dry, complex medical standards and diagnostic procedures into clean, quick-fire multiple choice layouts. I collaborated with subject matter experts to design quiz banks covering:

- Pulpal and periapical diagnoses.
- Endodontic instrumentation techniques.
- Post-treatment complications and emergency management.

The primary technical writing hurdle was ensuring that question explanations were concise yet didactically complete—helping students learn from incorrect answers without cluttering the mobile interface.
